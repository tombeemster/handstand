        Privacy
        
  
        
    
        The camera
        
    
        The camera picture is analysed on this phone, while you record. No video or photo is ever recorded, stored or sent anywhere.
        
    
        Your attempts
        
    
        Every attempt, its time and duration is stored on this phone only, in the browser. Clearing the browser's data, or Reset log, removes them. Export log is your only copy.
        
    
        Sharing anonymous totals
        
    
        Off unless you turn it on. When on, the app sends one summary at the end of each session: the date, how many attempts were timed automatically or by hand, near misses, attempts under 1 second, and that session's average, best and all-time record in milliseconds, plus the app version.
        
    
        It contains no name, no account, no device or session id, and no time of day. The summaries can't be linked to you, to your phone or to each other, which also means we can't find or delete a single one on request.
        
    
        Where it goes
        
    
        The summary goes to a Cloudflare Worker (Cloudflare, Inc.), which stores it with Supabase (Supabase, Inc.) in Frankfurt, Germany. As with any web request, Cloudflare receives your connection's IP address to deliver it; the Worker does not record it. Summaries are kept for [retention period] and then deleted.
        
    
        No tracking
        
    
        No cookies, no analytics or advertising tools, no fingerprinting.
        
    
        Filming
        
    
        Only film your own child, and only with her agreement.
