        Privacy
        
  
        
  
        
    
        Camera
        
    
        The camera is only used on this phone, while you record. Nothing is filmed, saved or sent.
        
    
        Attempts and settings
        
    
        Your attempts, what wasn't timed and the name you give stay on this phone. 'Delete all attempts' clears the entire log.
        
    
        Make sure to use 'Export (.csv)' to back up your attempts data.
        
    
        Sharing a record
        
    
        Tapping the record in your summary makes a picture on this phone, with the record, the date and the name if you gave one. It only goes where you send it.
        
    
        Sharing sessions
        
    
        Off by default. If you turn it on, the app sends a short summary after each session: the date, how many attempts and misses, and the average, best and record times.
        
    
        No name, no account, no ids, no time of day. Nothing links a summary to you, so we can't find or delete yours later.
        
    
        Where it goes
        
    
        Through Cloudflare to Supabase, stored in Europe. Cloudflare sees your IP address to deliver it, but it isn't saved.
        
    
        No tracking
        
    
        No cookies, no ads, no tracking tools.
        
    
        Filming
        
    
        Never film athletes without consent.

        Handstand
        
    
  
        
  
        
    
        
      
        Welcome to Handstand
        
      
        A web app that times each handstand automatically with the camera and logs the attempts.
        
      
        Tip: Save it to your home screen for easy access.
        
    
        
    
        
      
      
        Set up the phone
        
      
        Upright on a tripod, 2–4 m away and not low down. Keep the whole body in view, feet to raised hands.
        
      
        Your phone will ask for the camera: that's what times the handstand.
        
    
        
    
        
      
      
        Lighting and no clutter
        
      
        Good light and a plain background help tracking the handstand. Clear away anything that looks even remotely similar to a person.
        
      
        If it's too dark, a warning shows at the top of the camera.
        
    
        
    
        
      
      
        Privacy-first
        
      
        No video is ever stored or uploaded. Sharing session totals is off unless you turn it on.
